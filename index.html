AQUA KYC · OUR PRODUCT

One place where your bank talks to CKYC.

Your branch, your app and your loan desk all need the same customer records. Today each one is wired to the CKYC registry separately. AQuA KYC does it once, for all of them, under your bank's own registration.

  • Search & download find a customer and get their record, with the consent the registry requires
  • Create a record file a new customer, with duplicates put to an officer
  • Update & sync one change reaching the registry and every bank system
