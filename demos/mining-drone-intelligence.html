From flight hours to pit-floor P&L.

A 12-month drone programme at one open-pit iron-ore site: stockpile surveys, pit-wall monitoring and haul-road inspection, read as one dataset and then converted into five costed moves. The data is the diagnosis; the strategy is the treatment.

Headline insightTwo haul-road segments, H3 and H5, produce 61% of tyre-damage incidents and sit in the top defect-density band all four quarters. Regrading just those 3.1 km is the single cheapest lever on the site: ~$470k a year in tyre and fuel effect for ~$85k of works.

Insight. Alert spikes follow blasting weeks, as expected. The value is in the triage line: auto-routing alerts with imagery attached cut response from ~31 h to ~9 h. → Faster triage means benches re-open sooner after precautionary closures: ~$260k a year of avoided standby (Move 3).
