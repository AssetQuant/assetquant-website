How the threshold works

The slider above is the governance control

At or above thresholdPre-accepted by the model. Still visible, still reversible, still logged; the reviewer can override any of them.
Below thresholdHeld for review. The reviewer accepts, corrects in place, or rejects. The model's value is never written without that decision.
Always heldMonetary amounts above $1M and any date that drives a deadline, regardless of confidence. Some fields are too consequential to automate.
