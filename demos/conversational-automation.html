      Containment with a floor, not a ceiling

      In this illustrative scenario, the assistant is modelled to resolve 60–70% of routine requests and route the rest early. A customer can reach a person in one word at any point; containment is never achieved by trapping someone.

      Verify before you disclose or act

      Protective actions (freezing a card, revoking a session) can happen first; anything that discloses data or moves value waits for verification. The order is a rule, not a habit.

      Handoffs arrive with context

      When a person takes over they receive what was verified, what was tried, which systems were checked and a recommended next step, so the conversation does not start again.
