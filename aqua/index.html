We are building AQuA one piece at a time, starting with the job every bank has to do and nobody enjoys.

AQuA KYC

Your branch, your app and your loan desk all need the same customer records from the CKYC registry. Today each one is wired to it separately. AQuA KYC does it once, for all of them, under your bank's own registration — with each channel seeing only what it should, and one record of who did what.

How we build it.

The computer does the counting

Numbers, comparisons and reconciliation are done by code that gives the same answer twice. Nothing important is left to a model's guess.

Every answer shows its working

Where a figure came from, which record, which call, at what time. If it cannot be traced back, it does not go on the screen.

A person decides

Software can gather the evidence and put it in order. It does not approve, reject or change anything in a bank's systems on its own.
