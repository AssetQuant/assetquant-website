Four proposed KYC modules

Designed as a concept for integration-heavy banking environments; not a claim of CKYC network access, authorization or compliance certification.

01 · Verify

CKYC verification hub

Conceptual single verification interface across client channels and authorized sources.

02 · Integrate

KYC orchestration

Coordinate request validation, downstream checks and response normalization.

03 · Maintain

KYC lifecycle management

Track review, refresh and update workflows with clear ownership.

04 · Govern

Compliance and audit

Trace evidence, decisions, exception handling and review history.

Regulated KYC verification and CKYC integrations require applicable authorization, approved data access, security assessment and institution-specific compliance review. AQuA KYC is not presently offered as a live regulated verification service.

Potential workflow

  1. Receive a verification request from an authorized channel.
  2. Validate consent, permissions and request completeness.
  3. Route checks to approved systems where permitted.
  4. Normalize responses and flag exceptions for human review.
  5. Record the decision and supporting audit evidence.
