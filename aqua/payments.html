Eight planned Payments modules

A modular vision for operational awareness, investigation and controlled resolution.

01 · Data

Event ingestion

Receive and validate payment event records; preserve event lineage and auditability.

02 · Investigation

Transaction investigation

Correlate transaction history, statuses and related evidence for analyst review.

03 · Detection

Monitoring and detection

Explore abnormal patterns, failure spikes and emerging operational hotspots.

04 · Impact

Impact analytics

Quantify affected volume, value, channels and operational exposure with deterministic calculations.

05 · Operations

Incident management

Track triage, ownership, evidence, resolution and incident lifecycle.

06 · Intelligence

AI-assisted intelligence

Retrieve comparable incidents and suggest hypotheses with supporting evidence; humans decide.

07 · Visibility

Reporting and dashboards

Show operational trends, exception queues, channel health and resolution performance.

08 · Governance

Identity and audit

Record user actions, role-based access intent and human approval points.

How an investigation could work

  1. Ingest authorized operational event feeds.
  2. Detect unusual failure or exception patterns.
  3. Correlate evidence and calculate impact deterministically.
  4. Retrieve similar incidents and present hypotheses.
  5. Analyst reviews, decides and records the confirmed outcome.

Validation-first engineering

The existing local prototype accepts validated transaction events via Spring Boot and stores them in PostgreSQL. Detection, investigation and intelligence modules remain on the roadmap.
