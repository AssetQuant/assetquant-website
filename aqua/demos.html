Customer lookup

Getting a record out of the registry

A branch officer looks a customer up by PAN. The registry holds two records for them — one active, one an old duplicate.

  1. The request arrives and the channel's permissions are applied
  2. The registry returns both records, each with its status
  3. The customer's permission is taken before anything is released
  4. The trail is written — without the customer's details in it
Watch the lookup →
Address change

One change reaching every system

A customer changes their address in mobile banking. It has to reach the registry and four of the bank's own systems. One of them doesn't answer.

  1. The change is checked and approved by a named officer
  2. It goes to the registry, and the acknowledgement comes back
  3. Four systems are told; three confirm, the loan system stays silent
  4. Nothing is marked as done — it goes to a person instead
