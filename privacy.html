Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how AssetQuant handles personal information submitted through this website. It is intended to provide a clear, proportionate notice for our current website and enquiry workflow.

Information we receive

When you submit our enquiry form, we collect the name, email address, organisation, phone number (if supplied), enquiry category, timeline, message and consent confirmation. Please do not submit confidential banking data or passwords.

Why we use it

We use your email to review and respond to your enquiry and maintain appropriate business records. Please do not send sensitive credentials or unnecessary personal information.

Enquiry processing and service providers

Our website enquiry form uses Google Apps Script to receive enquiries, Google Sheets to store them privately and Google email services to notify our team. Google acts as a third-party service provider and may process data under its applicable terms. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We use enquiry information to respond to enquiries and manage follow-up; we do not sell enquiry information. We review stored enquiries and delete them when no longer necessary for the purpose collected, subject to applicable requirements.

Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, related business follow-up, legal obligations, dispute resolution and security. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the enquiry.

Your choices and requests

You may ask us to correct or delete personal information you submitted, withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis for processing, or raise a privacy concern. Email assetquantanalytics@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the current website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This business website is intended for professional users and is not directed to children. Please do not submit personal information about a child by email.

Indian data-protection framework

AssetQuant intends to operate this website consistently with applicable Indian data-protection requirements, including provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules as and when they apply. This policy is not a representation that provisions subject to phased commencement are already in force.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website, our service providers or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will identify the current version.

Contact

Email assetquantanalytics@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.
